# Dragon Block: Tenkaichi 0.3.0

## Added

### Look and sound
- A new art direction for the whole mod that mixes the Dragon Ball style with the blocky feel of the base game.
  - Menus and HUD use crisp pixel-art frames with an outline and bevel: gi-orange buttons, gi-blue panels, gold trim and red Dragon Ball stars, with orange-to-yellow outlined titles in the style of the series logo.
  - Menu backgrounds are a Dragon Ball sky with twinkling stars, a slowly turning four-star Dragon Ball and blocky clouds drifting by.
  - The HUD bars sit next to the base game's heart, hunger and armor icons, with new pixel icons for Ki and stamina; the bars are segmented and outlined.
  - Particles were redrawn as pixel art: ki sparks, flame tongues for the aura, hit stars, shock rings, dust puffs, lightning, speed lines and rock chunks.
  - Technique icons and the tab icons are pixel art: scouter, ki ball, Super Saiyan hair, Capsule and a close cross.
  - Ki blasts have a spinning cube at their core and beams a spinning square core, inside the usual round glow.
- **Full-screen character menu (U)**, laid out like a modern RPG character screen:
  - tab icons down the left side, your character large in the middle on a glowing pedestal in your aura color, the details panel on the right, the current tab in the title bar, BP and TP at the top and a close button at the top right;
  - drag the character to rotate it and scroll to zoom; the panel scrolls with the mouse wheel;
  - **Status**: every attribute on its own row with level, training progress, cost and upgrade button, your stat cap, racial traits and race description;
  - **Techniques**: the equipped slots with their keys, the known techniques with icons, and the techniques you can learn;
  - **Forms**: each transformation as a card marked by a Dragon Ball whose stars show its tier, with power, mastery, every requirement and the Trial button;
  - **Settings** as before; press **U** again to close.
- New sound design: punchier, anime-style hits, ki blasts, instant-transmission vanish, roaring auras and beams, with a drier, slightly retro finish.
  - Menus sound like a scouter: it boots up when the character menu opens, blips on tabs and clicks, plays a rising chime when a stat goes up and buzzes when you cannot afford it.
  - Base game sounds are layered in where they fit: the block under you on landings, dashes and dodges, shield sounds on guards and guard breaks, strong and critical hit sounds on heavy blows and clashes, the explosion sound under ki explosions, the wind burst under the sonic boom, the teleport sound under vanish, and the experience and level-up sounds when you level a stat or learn a technique.

### Transformations
- Transformations can be **equipped and unequipped** from the Forms tab of the character menu (U).
  - Transform (V) and power down (B) skip unequipped forms, so you can go straight from base to Super Saiyan 2, or keep only the forms you like.
  - Unequipping the form you are in brings you back to the nearest equipped one.
  - Every form starts equipped; your choice is saved with your character.

### Majin
- Majins are now **fat by default**, like Majin Buu: a big round belly, thick arms and short legs in baggy pants. The new **Fat** build is chosen automatically for a new Majin and can be changed in the character creator (only Majins can pick it).
  - The fat body wears Buu's outfit: an open vest with gold trim (accent color), white baggy pants (main color), a purple cape, yellow gloves and boots and the black belt with the Majin "M" buckle. With the Natural outfit only the skin shows.
  - Majin heads now have Buu's three steam holes on each side.
- **Three Majin Buu transformations**, unlocked like the Super Saiyan forms (Potential 8, 20 and 35, base BP 20,000, 90,000 and 250,000 and a fight against yourself in the form):
  - **Evil Buu**: thin and slightly taller, with grey skin, dark eyes with white pupils and a dark blue cape. Its trial takes place at Majin Buu's pink dome house.
  - **Super Buu**: tall (about 2.4 m) and muscular, shirtless with black and yellow armbands, baggy pants and black boots; red eyes. Its trial takes place in the Hyperbolic Time Chamber.
  - **Kid Buu**: small with a bigger head, shirtless like Super Buu; the fastest and strongest Buu form. Its trial takes place on the Sacred World of the Kai.
  - Each form has its own cutscene based on the anime:
    - Evil Buu: the rage builds up, steam jets out of the holes in your head, rises into a cloud that gathers in the sky, comes back down over you and leaves Evil Buu grinning in the smoke.
    - Super Buu: chocolate flies into your mouth, you swallow and laugh, pink smoke pours out of your body and swallows you while you grow, then it is blown away to reveal the towering Super Buu.
    - Kid Buu: steam spews from your head, your body balloons into a hulking giant, then shrinks fast, flickering between forms, until a steam blast leaves Kid Buu cackling.
  - New sounds for the cutscenes: hissing steam, a wicked laugh (higher-pitched for Kid Buu), a gulp and a rubbery stretch.
  - The Buu bodies are also drawn on your arms in first person.

### Masters
- **Master Roshi** was remodelled after his canonical look: 165 cm tall, bald with white tufts at the sides, thin white brows, red-framed sunglasses, a long white beard and moustache, an orange Hawaiian shirt with purple triangles and black letters, white shorts, green flip-flops and the purple Turtle Shell on his back with its rope strap.

## Changed
- When a sparring session or dodge drill with a Master ends, the Master returns to the exact spot where the training started (with an instant-transmission vanish), even if he was knocked far away or the world was reloaded mid-session.
- During transformation cutscenes the mouse no longer turns the camera and your first-person hand and the crosshair are hidden.
- The **Voice** option was removed from character creation and from saved characters, since it had no effect.
- The character creator and the training menu use the new style, with the character on the glowing pedestal; menu titles shrink to fit small windows.
